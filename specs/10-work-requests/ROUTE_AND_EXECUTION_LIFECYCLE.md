# Route & Execution Lifecycle

## Main route

At creation, an authenticated active internal ActorProfile with explicit compatible `CREATE_WORK_REQUEST` and same-Company parent access selects an active Originating Division and supplies an ordered `intermediateDivisionIds` list with at least one existing, active, same-Tenant Division. A creator who actively leads the Originating Division may let the server bind that exact DivisionLead responsibility; another authorized business creator supplies the exact active `responsibleOriginatingDivisionLeadId` for that Division. The system validates and persists that exact responsibility, permits repeated IDs, and derives two immutable tail occurrences: `ORIGIN_FINAL_REVIEW` for the originating Division and `CLIENT`. Neither tail is supplied by the client. Company-wide business visibility or creation permission never grants execution authority, and another Lead of that Division does not inherit route authority.

Each route occurrence has stable identity, route version, ordinal, Division reference where applicable, kind (`ORIGIN_START`, `INTERMEDIATE`, `ORIGIN_FINAL_REVIEW`, `CLIENT`), and immutable activation/completion history. Normal forward is derived exclusively from the active route occurrence and its next occurrence in the approved route version. It cannot select an arbitrary Division.

## Independent presentation visibility

Main Route and Internal Division Workflow are independent presentation/authorization concerns. Main Route returns its own actor-specific `workflowVisibility.canViewMainWorkflow`, derived from route-level Work Request authorization, and remains the authority for Main Workflow presentation. An originating responsible actor who still satisfies Main Route access retains Main visibility after another Division becomes current; Team-level and participating Member contexts remain Main false. `GET /:id/internal-cycles` separately discovers only the caller's authorized own-Division cycle or cycles; it is not limited to Main Route's current `divisionCycleId`, so an authorized participating Division actor can read that actor's completed or current own Division workflow without gaining another Division's workflow. Internal Cycle access is derived separately from the caller's authorized Division/Team/Member context. A 403 for another Division's Internal Cycle neither changes nor signals Main Route visibility, and must not be used to hide Main Workflow. This metadata-only contract does not modify route steps, route occurrences, route ordering, current responsibility, Division forward, Client stage, or any execution lifecycle fact.

## Amendments

An amendment is available only to the exact bound Responsible Originating Division Lead and is an append-only route version linked to its predecessor and actor/event metadata. It carries a new ordered plan for only unstarted future intermediate occurrences, then derives its own origin-final/client tail. Completed occurrences and the active occurrence are copied as immutable historical context, never changed or deleted. Optimistic base-version comparison plus serializable write prevents competing amendments. A responsibility transfer is a separate auditable fact and never an amendment side effect.

## Division, Team, and Member execution

Activating a Division route occurrence creates exactly one Division execution cycle for that occurrence. Repeated Division occurrences create new cycles even when the same Division ID appears again.

Before an active Division cycle begins Team work, its exact Division Lead defines a complete, ordered Team sequence. Team steps have stable ID, ordinal, Team, lifecycle facts, and one active step. Once the sequence starts it is frozen; no additional Team may be appended. Teams complete sequentially.

Before an active Team begins Member work, its exact Team Lead defines ordered Member steps. A Member step has stable ID, ordinal, Member, lifecycle facts, and one active step. The Team Lead may append a new unstarted Member step during the active Team cycle; completed steps are immutable and order is never rewritten. A Member submits only their active step. The final Member submits to Team Lead; the Team Lead completes that Team before the next Team starts. Completing final Team returns control to the Division cycle; completing final Division permits normal main-route forward.

## Derived position and events

Persist append-only lifecycle events for creation, amendment, activation, sequence definition, Member append, submit, Team completion, Division completion, forward, and pause/resume. Derive main route position, active cycle, active Team step, active Member step, and permitted actions from the latest valid facts plus no-open-revision rule. Mutable performance projections are permitted only if transactionally derived from those facts.

## Archive and restore execution freeze

Archive is a separate, restorable aggregate lifecycle overlay. It may freeze an active/in-progress Work Request without changing Main Workflow or Internal Division Workflow facts: route steps and ordering, active occurrence/cycle/TeamStep/MemberStep, current responsibility, Client stage, Team sequence/order, Member sequence/order, and derived normal projection remain exactly as persisted. `WORK_REQUEST_ARCHIVED` records the actor, time, optional reason, and pre-existing workflow-state snapshot; `WORK_REQUEST_RESTORED` records the corresponding restoration. Neither event rewrites historical events nor advances, completes, cancels, or resets any workflow state.

An archived request is read-only. The service must reject every normal execution mutation before a write: Team/Member sequence definition, Member append/submit, Team completion, Division forward, route amendment, revision request/resubmit, Request Info create/respond, communication send/reply, client submission/decision/reconsideration, and all other workflow mutations. Read projections remain authorized by their existing tenant/scope/relationship rules. Restore removes only the archive overlay, so permitted execution resumes at the exact pre-archive position; it never creates a fresh route, Division cycle, TeamStep, MemberStep, document, version, revision, or assignment.

## Internal Division Workflow (derived projection)

The ordered Team sequence is the sole source of truth for the independent Internal Division Workflow. On successful save of an active Division cycle's existing Team sequence, the system automatically derives a Division-level projection with automatic boundaries: `Division Start -> TeamStep 1 -> ... -> TeamStep N -> Division Return`. The Division Lead selects Teams only; neither Division boundary is selectable, persisted as caller input, or created through a separate workflow builder, modal, action, or second sequence.

This is a Division-level visualization/execution projection of existing responsibility, not a Main Workflow route extension and not a second execution engine. It does not append to, replace, route through, or alter Main Workflow steps, Division forward behavior, route progression, Client stage, or Main Workflow state transitions. Main Workflow remains exactly its existing route occurrence lifecycle; each Division cycle independently derives its own Internal Workflow from its own Team sequence.

The state of the projection is derived from existing Division-cycle/Team-step responsibility facts. With `Team A -> Team B -> Team C`, while Team A is active, Division Start is completed, Team A is current, later Teams and Division Return are upcoming. When Team A completes, Team B becomes current. Once the final Team completes, all Team nodes are completed and Division Return is current, representing responsibility returned to the Division. Member sequences remain nested inside their Team; they are never flattened into the Division-level projection.

If a valid future business flow changes an authoritative Team sequence before its existing freeze rules prohibit that change, the projection reflects that sequence and nothing else. Revisions may temporarily move responsibility under their preserved requester/source/resume rules, but never mutate the normal Internal Workflow Team sequence or its derived normal projection.

### Sequential Team execution and Division return

The Division-level responsible actor owns setup when the Division occurrence becomes active. Its ordered Team sequence determines the internal execution order; the first Team is the first internal execution target. The internal projection remains `Division Start → Team 1 → ... → Team N → Division End`. With no Team sequence it remains `Division Start → Division End`: the Division-level actor handles the Division phase directly and no TeamStep is invented.

At most one TeamStep is active/current. Its Team Lead manages that Team's own ordered Member sequence; later Teams remain upcoming. A Member submits only the active MemberStep. If another Member remains, responsibility passes to that Member. The last Member's submission returns responsibility to the same Team Lead and leaves that TeamStep current; it does not activate the next Team or forward the Division. A legitimate Member appended at the end before Team completion executes in sequence before responsibility returns to the Team Lead. Append is ordinary sequence extension, not revision.

The responsible Team Lead explicitly invokes the existing canonical `COMPLETE_TEAM_STEP` action after all of that Team's Members have submitted, or directly when the active Team has no Member sequence. This is distinct from Member submission. Completion of a non-final Team completes its TeamStep, activates only the next TeamStep, and transfers responsibility to that Team's Lead, who manages that Team's own Member sequence; no prior Team's Members are reused. Completion of the final Team returns responsibility to the Division-level responsible actor and makes Division End current; it does not activate the next Main Workflow Division.

The Division-level actor reviews the work and alone performs the separate Division `FORWARD` after all Teams complete, advancing to the next approved Main Route occurrence. The internal Team execution may record Division-cycle completion when the last Team completes; this internal completion is not Main Route forwarding. The Division End becomes completed when the Division occurrence is forwarded. Revision remains a separate process and does not mutate either normal sequence; Request Info and document/version links are unaffected.

Member A's submission transfers execution to Member B, or back to the Team Lead after the last Member, without deleting A's legitimate participation relationship. A's completed step no longer permits normal Member submission; authorized read and eligible collaboration access remain available through later Team and Division progression. The same applies to every completed Team Member. A later revision is controlled by the existing revision-specific responsibility and authorization rules, not by this read relationship.

Implementation concern to resolve in future work: `workRequestVisibilityWhere` is shared by Work Request list and detail reads. The Member predicate now recognizes non-cancelled MemberStep participation, but Team and Division route predicates still rely on incomplete TeamSteps or Division cycles and can hide a former responsible actor after handoff when no other visibility branch applies. Read/collaboration authorization must recognize legitimate Work Request-specific historical responsibility under the approved tenant, scope, permission, and resource constraints; execution authorization must remain current-responsibility based. The implementation must establish the actual persisted actor-to-Work-Request relationship rather than infer historical responsibility solely from a current Team or Division title. This note is diagnostic and does not change implementation.

### Responsibility handoff and retained participation

Current execution responsibility and historical Work Request participation are separate authorization concepts. After Team A Lead completes Team A and Team B becomes current, Team A Lead retains authorized Work Request list/detail and own-Division Internal Workflow reads and eligible collaboration through the persisted legitimate Work Request-specific responsibility relationship. The completed Team does not expose `COMPLETE_TEAM_STEP` again. Last-Team completion, return to Division Lead, Division Forward, and later Main Workflow progression do not alone remove a legitimate prior participant's authorized reads. Division-level actors likewise retain read/history access after forwarding when their own persisted Work Request-specific Division responsibility supports it. Completed Member retention remains unchanged.

The historical relationship must identify the actor's legitimate participation or responsibility for this specific Work Request. A current Team or Division title, a TeamStep or DivisionCycle alone without a demonstrated actor relationship, and permission alone do not prove that a person historically held responsibility. Existing cancelled, invalid, revoked, tenant, scope, and resource constraints still apply. Historical access grants no execution action unless a separate current workflow assignment and its existing authorization permit it. The Request Info recipient matrix and pair/lifecycle rules are unchanged; participation alone does not create an eligible recipient, and archive restrictions remain in force.

### Proposed responsibility-history write boundary

The additive responsibility-history proposal in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` records exact Team and Division actors transactionally with the existing responsibility assignments and supported transfers. A Team or Division completion ends the corresponding interval without erasing its Work Request participation proof. Origin, intermediate, final-review and genuine return-to-Division responsibility use their own exact cycle context; existing valid multiple Division Leads remain supported. Unknown legacy responsibility is not reconstructed from current organizational leadership. This is a persistence and read-authorization addition; it does not change execution order, handoffs, projections or the separate current-responsibility guards.

### Same-transaction history integration

The future Team/Division responsibility writers use the shared transaction-aware port specified in `DATA_CONTRACT.md` inside the owning existing unit of work. Authorization-grade history never depends solely on a post-commit event. Existing current workflow execution, Team/Division transitions and projections remain authoritative and unchanged; historical reads are enabled only after writers, conservative backfill and RLS verification are complete. The prepared migration needs the specified provenance/idempotency revision before application.
